How to play

The numbers on the ledges

A ledge's number is what it costs in air to move onto it. They are rolled fresh every game: ledge 1 is always 1–2, the middle of a trench runs 2–5, and the bottom third runs 3–6 — the floor is never cheap. So each board is a different route to plan.

Paying for a move

You hold 5 air cards, refilled after every move. To drop a diver one ledge, discard any combination summing to that ledge's number exactly — no overpaying. A ledge glows when your current hand can pay it. If nothing is payable, your turn is spent swapping the whole hand for a fresh one.

The line — this is the game

Divers sharing a ledge are clipped to one line. When you descend, every diver stacked above yours comes with you, free. So each move you pay for is partly a gift to a rival — and landing on top of someone is the cheapest way down, if they keep moving.

Treasure, and running out of it

The first diver to a trench floor takes the richest token; later arrivals take what is left. When the last token goes, the trench is stripped — it closes, and every diver still inside surfaces with nothing, wasting every card spent getting there.

Winning

The game ends when someone surfaces their last diver; everyone gets one final turn. Divers still in the water score nothing. Most treasure wins.

Sunken Hold

A deep-sea salvage race

  1. Spend air cards to drop a diver one ledge. You must pay a ledge's number exactly.
  2. Divers sharing a ledge are clipped to one line — when you descend, everyone stacked above you comes too, free.
  3. First to a wreck takes the richest treasure. When the last token goes, the trench is stripped and everyone still inside surfaces with nothing.
